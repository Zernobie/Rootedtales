## 3. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY (COPPA COMPLIANCE)

We are committed to protecting children's privacy and comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

### 3.1 Parental Consent

**For Children Under 13:**
- We require verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information
- Parents provide consent through email verification
- Parents can review, modify, or delete their child's information at any time
- Parents can refuse further collection or use of their child's information

### 3.2 Information We Collect from Children

We collect only the minimum information necessary:
- Username (may be pseudonymous)
- Age range (not exact birthdate)
- Reading progress and preferences
- Achievement data
- Device information (for app functionality)

**We do NOT collect from children:**
- Full name (unless provided by parent)
- Exact date of birth
- Precise location
- Photos or videos (beyond optional avatar)
- Social security numbers or other sensitive identifiers

### 3.3 How We Use Children's Information

Children's information is used only to:
- Provide the reading experience
- Track progress and achievements
- Sync across devices (if applicable)
- Improve app functionality
- Comply with legal obligations

### 3.4 Third-Party Access to Children's Information

We do not share children's personal information with third parties except:
- Service providers bound by strict confidentiality (e.g., cloud storage)
- As required by law
- With parental consent

### 3.5 Parental Rights

Parents have the right to:
- Review their child's personal information
- Request deletion of their child's information
- Refuse further collection of their child's information
- Contact us at hub@xenwinx.com for any child privacy concerns

### 3.6 Parental Controls

Parents can:
- Access and manage their child's account
- Control notification settings
- Disable in-app purchases
- Monitor reading activity
- Set age-appropriate content filters

Your data is stored on secure servers provided by reputable cloud service providers, which may be located in various countries. We ensure all data transfers comply with applicable privacy laws.
